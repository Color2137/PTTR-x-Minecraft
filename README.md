<h2>What works</h2>

**Movement:** Minecraft Movement on PTTR's Terrain,Props,Buildings anything that has a colider in PTTR it has a colision with the
Minecraft Player

**Blocks:** you can place and break them the Blocks are Rendered in Mincraft and Displayed in PTTR and synchronized with PTTR
so the TNT and other stuff works but sometimes theres bugs so enemies can walk inside those blocks

**Combat:** hit PTTR NPCs with any Minecraft weapon the damage by the enemies and player are translated from MC to PTTR
so if you have 0 hearts you just die in PTTR or if you hit many times the enemy then he would die

**Camera:** the F5 from 1st person to 3rd person works just like in minecraft 

**Multiplayer:** if you try to play with your friend both you could have some technical problems

**Scenarios:** so the PTTRs Scenarios,Arena,Beneath,Player made levels work and it works like if you enter them it creates new world in your minecraft
instance in folder "saves" and theres where the blocks and some other minecraft stuff happens
